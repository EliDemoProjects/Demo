# Viewing Results

The Checkmarx SCA web portal (UI) shows data for all scans run in your tenant account (subject to access control for the individual user). Checkmarx SCA shows aggregated results for all Projects in your account and also for individual Projects. You can also view detailed info about the packages that were identified in your Project and the risks associated with those packages.

On the **Dashboard** screen, you can view general info about the Projects in your account. You can also drill down to view the **Project** page for a specific Project, which shows detailed data about the risks identified in that Project as well as detailed info about the packages and risks that were identified in the latest scan of the Project.

On the **Global Inventory & Risks** screen, you can access info about all packages and the associated risks that were identified in any of the Projects in your account. You can also drill down to open the **Project** page showing the **Packages** or **Risks** tabs for specific packages or risks.
